About Annepeace

From Githurai to the global stage, without losing her center of gravity

Annepeace Alwala leads Global Service Delivery at Sama, the ethical AI company that trains and validates data for some of the world's largest technology organisations. Her career began in call-centre operations and customer experience before she stepped into global operations leadership — always staying close to the people whose work she leads.
